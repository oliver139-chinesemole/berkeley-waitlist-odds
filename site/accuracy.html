What could not be tested on Fall 2026

  • The first week of instruction. Berkeleytime recorded nothing for any section from Aug 19 to Sep 1, 2026, which covers the end of the adjustment period and the first week of class. Nobody who joined in the adjustment period can be scored, and no joiner from before the hole can be followed to the first day of class or to the last automatic waitlist run, so the two runs headed "by the last automatic waitlist run" score only joiners from instruction, who joined after the hole. The registrar published no last-run date for Fall 2026; the backtest takes Sep 11, the last day to add without a fee, which is the day Spring 2027's last run falls on. Fall 2026 validates clearing in Phase 1, in Phase 2 and in instruction after the hole; "Which joiners could be scored, by phase", under each run, gives the shares.
  • Reserved seats. A section can hold seats for a category the joiner is not in; the counts do not say who was eligible, so the estimates treat the queue as first-in-first-out.
  • The Cox model across the Phase 1 to Phase 2 shift. Fit on Phase 1 joiners, it under-predicts Phase 2 clearing; its scores are shown for honesty, but the pages use the Kaplan-Meier curves, not the model.
